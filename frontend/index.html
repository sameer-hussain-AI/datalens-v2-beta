MEET DATA LENS V2.0

Making data analysis simpler and smarter.

Data Lens is a modern data analytics workspace designed to help users explore, understand, clean, visualize and analyze their datasets in one place.

About Data Lens

Working with data often involves multiple tools and repetitive tasks. Data Lens brings essential data analysis capabilities together in one intuitive workspace. Upload your dataset, explore its structure, identify quality issues, clean your data and discover insights through visualizations and statistical analysis.

What you can do

Explore the core capabilities of Data Lens.

01. Data Overview

Understand dataset dimensions, columns, data types and key information.

02. Data Quality

Identify missing values, duplicates and data completeness issues.

03. Data Cleaning

Apply cleaning operations and export your prepared dataset.

04. Visualization

Create charts to explore patterns, distributions and relationships.

05. Statistical Analysis

Explore statistical measures and understand numerical data.

06. Multiple File Formats

Work with CSV, XLSX and XLS datasets.
